Standard frontend websites or business landing pages typically take 5 to 10 working days. Complete full-stack web applications with authentication, databases, and custom payment gateways take roughly 2 to 4 weeks depending on complexity. For standard projects, we use a transparent milestone model: 50% upfront deposit to initiate design and architectural sprints, and the remaining 50% only after you approve the final working site on our staging server before deployment to your live domain. Local transfers (Naira) and international payments (USD/GBP via Wise or Stripe) are supported.
