Privacy & Analytics Notice

We use minimal, privacy-first session analytics to understand how visitors explore the portfolio. Zero third-party ads or cross-site tracking.

🛡️

Privacy & Data Protection

Compliance: Built in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and international data privacy principles.

What is tracked: Anonymous session identifiers, country and city (derived from serverless edge headers), device type, viewed sections, and button interactions (WhatsApp, call, form).

Weather-Responsive Theme: When permitted, approximate regional coordinates derived from edge headers are used once to adapt the page theme to local weather and daylight via Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com). No coordinates or IPs are ever stored.

What is NOT tracked: We never store plain IP addresses (salted SHA-256 hash only), exact GPS coordinates, or cross-site tracking cookies. No third-party ad networks or trackers are loaded.

Data Retention: Session and notification logs are automatically purged after 90 days.
